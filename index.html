Two ways to power a home

Conventional energy relies on burning finite fossil fuels. Eco-friendly sources harvest energy that nature renews every day, without the emissions.

Conventional sources

Coal

Burned in power plants for electricity. High carbon output and heavy air pollution.

Oil

Used for heating and backup generation. A finite resource with volatile prices.

Natural Gas

Cleaner than coal but still a fossil fuel that emits CO₂ when combusted.

Eco-friendly sources

Solar

Photovoltaic panels convert daylight into free, zero-emission electricity.

Wind

Turbines turn breezes into power, day and night, with no fuel cost.

Biomass

Organic waste becomes renewable energy, closing the carbon loop.
